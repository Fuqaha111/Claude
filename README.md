## AI Fluency for builders (Claude Academy)

### Introduction to AI Fluency

AI Fluency: the ability to engage with AI systems in ways that are efficient, effective, ethical and safe

#### Course Framework

AI Fluency Framework --> Delegation --> Description --> Discernment --> Diligence 

4D Framework
- Delegation: when should humans do the work and when should AI
- Description: How do we communicate clearly with AI systems
- Discernment: How do we evaluate what AI gives us
- Diligence: How do we ensure our interaction with AI is responsible, transparent and safe

### The 4D Framework

- The inner loop (Description and discernment) guides daily interaction with AI. the outer loop (Delegation and Diligence) guides bigger decisions about when and how to use AI

#### Description 

- communicate effectively with AI systems. This goes beyond writing good prompts

Example 

- instead your prompt being write a summary of this report. Try summarize this report for non-technical executives focusing on the three highest-impacting finding in under 300 words

#### Discernment 

Ask yourself 3 questions

- is it accurate?
- does the language match what I am looking for?
- How did the AI get there?

This is why description and discernment are called the inner loop, because you describe what you want, evaluate what you get then refine your description and so on until you reach a desired output

#### Delegation 

- deciding what work should be done by humans? what work should be done by AI? and how to distribute tasks between them?

Ask yourself 
- is this a routine task?
- is human naunce required
- is confidintial data involved

#### diligance 

- Be intintional about AI assistance
- Be honest about AI's role in your work
- Verify accuracy of AI outputs
