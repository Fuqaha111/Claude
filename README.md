## AI Fluency for builders

### Introduction to AI Fluency

AI Fluency: the ability to engage with AI systems in ways that are efficient, effective, ethical and safe

#### Course Framework

AI Fluency Framework --> Delegation --> Description --> Discernment --> Diligence 

4D Framework
- Delegation: when should humans do the work and when should AI
- Description: How do we communicate clearly with AI systems
- Discernment: How do we evaluate what AI gives us
- Diligence: How do we ensure our interaction with AI is responsible, transparent and safe
