### Introduction to AI Fluency

AI Fluency: the ability to engage with AI systems in ways that are efficient, effective, ethical and safe

#### Course Framework

AI Fluency Framework --> Delegation --> Description --> Discernment --> Diligence 

4D Framework
- Delegation: when should humans do the work and when should AI
- Description: How do we communicate clearly with AI systems
- Discernment: How do we evaluate what AI gives us
- Diligence: How do we ensure our interaction with AI is responsible, transparent and safe

### The 4D Framework

- The inner loop (Description and discernment) guides daily interaction with AI. the outer loop (Delegation and Diligence) guides bigger decisions about when and how to use AI

#### Description 

- communicate effectively with AI systems. This goes beyond writing good prompts

Example 

- instead your prompt being write a summary of this report. Try summarize this report for non-technical executives focusing on the three highest-impacting finding in under 300 words

#### Discernment 

Ask yourself 3 questions

- is it accurate?
- does the language match what I am looking for?
- How did the AI get there?

This is why description and discernment are called the inner loop, because you describe what you want, evaluate what you get then refine your description and so on until you reach a desired output

#### Delegation 

- deciding what work should be done by humans? what work should be done by AI? and how to distribute tasks between them?

Ask yourself 
- is this a routine task?
- is human naunce required
- is confidintial data involved

#### diligance 

- Be intintional about AI assistance
- Be honest about AI's role in your work
- Verify accuracy of AI outputs


### AI Capailities and limitations

#### Generative AI

- artificial intellegence systems that can create new content instead of just analyzing existing data

#### LLM

- large language models are a prominant type of generative AI
- they are called LLM's because they are trained to predict and genrate human language and large because they contain billions of parameters

#### The three pillars that made LLMs possible 

- Algorithms: while neural networks have been around for decades the devlopment of the transformer architecture in 2017 was a game changer, this architecture excels at proccessing sequences of texts while maintaining relationship between words across long passages

- Data: the explosion of digital data provided the essential raw material for training

- Computation: massive increases in computational power maid it possible to train these complex models on all that data

#### scalling laws

-  as models grow larger and are trained on more data with more computing power, their performance improved. more surprisingly, research found that entire new capabilities began to merge as the models grow larger. like reasoning 

#### Two training stages

- Pretrianing: LLMs analyze patterns across billions of text examples, at this stage the model builds a complex map of language and knoweldge. At this stage the model is shown a text and asked to predict what comes next.

- Fine-tuning: where the model learns to follow instructions, provide helpful responses and avoid generating harmful content. This often involves feedback from humans to improve models performance, which usses rewards and penalties to shape model's behaviour.


There is also a practical limit to what the LLM can consider at once which is called AI context window (your prompts, AI responses, any info youve shared)

#### What makes genrative AI powerful 

- it ability to proccess vast amount information during training
- its in context learning ability that lets it adapt to new task and information 
- emergent capabilities that arise form scale
